ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

학회 소개
연구와 현장의 만남

전문대학의 응용연구가 기업의 실제 문제를 만나고, 그 결과가
새로운 기술개발과 사업 기회로 이어지는 자리입니다.

Co-R&BD의 Co는 College와 Cooperation을 함께 뜻합니다. 전문기술석사 연구, 산학공동기술개발과제의 성과, 기업의 기술수요를 한 공간에서 연결합니다.

College + Cooperation → Research & Business Development
01연구성과 공유

현장 문제를 해결하기 위해 수행한 연구 과정과 실증 결과, 기술적 성과를 발표하고 확산합니다.

02기술수요 연결

산업체의 현장 애로사항을 공유하고, 이를 해결할 수 있는 대학의 전문기술과 연구성과를 함께 모색합니다.

03후속과제 발굴

성과 발표와 기술 상담을 바탕으로 기업·대학 간 협력 가능성을 확인하고, 공동과제 기획과 후속 협의로 연결합니다.

Conference information in English ↗

T-VET Co-R&BD Conference 2026 brings together applied research from professional technical master's programs and industry–college collaborative technology development. The inaugural meeting aims to connect research outcomes with industry needs and future joint projects.

Call for Papers & Cases. Submissions are invited across four main tracks: (1) Professional Technical Master's — Engineering, (2) Professional Technical Master's — Natural Sciences, (3) Industry–College Joint Technology Development Projects, and (4) Other Industry–Academia Convergence Projects. Accepted papers will be scheduled into 3 Oral Sessions (minimum 20 papers) and 1 Poster Session (minimum 30 papers).

Submit a 2–4-page extended abstract for research, a 2-page practice case, or a 1-page work-in-progress poster or demonstration summary in Korean or English. At least one author must be affiliated with a college or be a collaborator in its joint project. Review and presentation require information cleared for disclosure.

Conference: January 20–21, 2027 at Eastern Campus, Ulsan College. General attendee application deadline: January 5, 2027, 18:00 KST (registration fee KRW 100,000 per non-presenting attendee and KRW 200,000 per presenter, by bank transfer; full refund for cancellations by January 13). The application page shows the current registration status. After acceptance, presenters register on the same application page by choosing the presenter category (open until the final paper and presenter registration deadline); the presenter fee is KRW 200,000 with the same refund policy. Accommodation (호텔현대 바이 라한 울산, 타니베이 호텔) is arranged according to a demand survey; early registrants can book at a discounted rate, while late registrants must arrange their own accommodation. Paper schedule: submissions open November 30; deadline December 21; notification January 4, 2027; revision resubmission deadline January 8, 2027; final paper and presenter registration deadline January 13, 2027. Paper dates and room details await confirmation.
