01 / ABOUT

연구의 성과를
현장의 변화로.

전문대학의 응용연구가 기업의 실제 문제를 만나고, 그 결과가 새로운 기술개발과 사업 기회로 이어지는 자리입니다.

Co-R&BD의 Co는 College와 Cooperation을 함께 뜻합니다. 전문기술석사 연구, 산학공동기술개발과제의 성과, 기업의 기술수요를 한 공간에서 연결합니다.

Conference information in English ↗

Co-R&BD Conference 2026 brings together applied research from professional technical master's programs and industry–college collaborative technology development. The inaugural meeting aims to connect research outcomes with industry needs and future joint projects.

Call for Papers & Practice Cases. Topics include applied master's research, outcomes of industry–college collaborative technology development projects, interdisciplinary industrial technologies, and master's program education and operation.

Submit a 2–4-page extended abstract for research, a 2-page practice case, or a 1-page work-in-progress poster or demonstration summary in Korean or English. At least one author must be affiliated with a college or be a collaborator in its joint project. Review and presentation require information cleared for disclosure.

Conference: December 17–18, 2026 at Dongbu Campus, Ulsan College. General attendee application deadline: October 30, 2026, 18:00 KST. Presenters register separately after acceptance. Proposed paper schedule: submission deadline November 6; notification November 20; final paper and presenter registration deadline November 27. Paper dates, room details, submission links, and organizers await confirmation.
